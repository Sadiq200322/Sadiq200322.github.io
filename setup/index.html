Set up Neupulse C

Four short guides cover pads, placement, first pairing, and a strap change. Do them in order the first time.

Gel pads

Applying gel pads

A fresh pair goes on the contact points before every session. Press them on flat so the whole pad touches the device. Peel the backing only when you are ready to wear it.

Placement

Placing the device

Wear it on the underside of the right wrist, slightly toward the thumb. The strap should be secure but not tight. Keep watches and bracelets on the other wrist.

Full guide

Setting up Neupulse C

Charge with the USB-C cable. Open Neutrack C, pair over Bluetooth, then choose intensity and session length. After that, the button on the device starts a session.

Full guide

Changing the strap

Unclip the strap from both lugs, line up the new strap, and click it home until it sits flush. A larger black strap is available if the standard one is too small.
